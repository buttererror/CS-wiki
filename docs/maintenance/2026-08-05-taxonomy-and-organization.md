# 2026-08-05 Taxonomy and Organization Pass

## Summary

Reorganized the CS Wiki around clearer conceptual ownership. The work focused
on making the repository easier to browse and maintain by separating stable
subject areas from notes that still need review.

This was repository maintenance and knowledge organization work, not a product
release, changelog entry, or task-completion record.

## What Changed

- Clarified the wiki's major subject areas and their relationships.
- Improved navigation and landing-page structure across the root README and
  topic indexes.
- Moved scattered notes into temporary review areas under `miscellaneous/`.
- Established `security/` and identity/access-management notes as a dedicated
  subject area.
- Reinforced that `miscellaneous/` is a review inbox, not a conceptual
  category.

## Why

The repo already contained useful learning material, but some pages were
organized by historical location rather than by field, scope, or abstraction
level. This pass made the structure better match the concepts the wiki is
trying to explain.

## Follow-Up

- Continue reviewing pages under `miscellaneous/`.
- Move stable material into canonical subject directories when its real scope
  is clear.
- Keep README navigation and landing pages updated as notes graduate from
  temporary review areas.
